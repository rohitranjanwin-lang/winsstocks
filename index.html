What is SIP?

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is a disciplined way to invest a fixed amount regularly (usually monthly) into mutual funds or ETFs. It allows investors to benefit from rupee cost averaging and the power of compounding over time.

SIP vs Lumpsum Investment

Investment Style

SIP: Monthly/Regular

Lumpsum: One-time

Volatility Handling

SIP: Better (Rupee Cost Averaging)

Lumpsum: Higher Risk in Market Timing

Recommended For

SIP: Beginners, salaried people

Lumpsum: Experienced investors

Flexibility

SIP: Can pause or change amount

Lumpsum: Less flexible
